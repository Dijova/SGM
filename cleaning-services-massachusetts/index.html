How to Keep Your Floors Clean and Shiny Between Professional Cleanings

Floors are the visual foundation of any space. However, keeping them spotless between deep cleanings can be complicated. Here are some practical tips to keep them looking great without damaging them.

Wood Floor Care

  • Use a dry or barely damp microfiber mop
  • Avoid excess water (it can make the wood swell)
  • Use neutral cleaners made for wood
  • Place mats at entrances to keep dirt out

Ceramic or Porcelain Floor Care

  • Sweep or vacuum frequently to remove debris
  • Use warm water with a neutral detergent
  • Avoid highly acidic cleaners that dull the shine

Vinyl or Laminate Floors

  • Do not use steam
  • Use gentle products applied with a damp cloth
  • Do not drag furniture without protection

What to Avoid in All Cases

  • Abrasive products (like undiluted bleach)
  • Hard brushes or metal sponges
  • Excess water or prolonged moisture

Recommended Frequency

Do a light cleaning daily in high-traffic areas and a deep cleaning at least once a month with a professional service.

Common Office Cleaning Mistakes and How to Avoid Them (Especially When Receiving Clients)

Office cleaning goes beyond wiping surfaces: it is a key part of your company's image. However, many businesses make mistakes that affect health, order and how professional the place looks. Here are the most frequent ones and how to avoid them.

Using the Wrong Products

Each surface requires a specific product. Using disinfectants on desks without knowing their composition can damage them or leave residue. The same applies to floors and bathrooms.

Ignoring "Invisible" Areas

Switches, handles, keyboards, phones and the areas under desks are often overlooked, but they accumulate bacteria and affect hygiene.

Cleaning Only When Dirt Is Visible

Preventive cleaning avoids build-up, odors and the spread of germs. Waiting until dirt is visible is a reactive and inefficient approach.

No Professional Cleaning Routine

General tidying by office staff does not replace a professional service. Without the right equipment or training, time is wasted and deep results are not guaranteed.
