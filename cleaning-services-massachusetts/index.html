How to Keep Your Floors Clean and Shiny Between Professional Cleanings

Floors are the visual foundation of any space. However, keeping them spotless between deep cleanings can be complicated. Here are some practical tips to keep them looking great without damaging them.

Wood Floor Care

  • Use dry or barely damp microfiber mop
  • Avoid excess water (can cause wood swelling)
  • Use neutral cleaners specific for wood
  • Place mats at entrances to prevent dirt

Ceramic or Porcelain Floor Care

  • Sweep or vacuum frequently to remove debris
  • Use warm water with neutral detergent
  • Avoid highly acidic cleaners that deteriorate shine

Vinyl or Laminate Floors

  • Don't use steam
  • Use gentle products and apply with damp cloth
  • Don't drag furniture without protection

What to Avoid in All Cases

  • Abrasive products (like pure bleach)
  • Hard brushes or metal sponges
  • Excess water or prolonged moisture

Recommended Frequency

Clean superficially daily if there's high traffic and perform deep cleaning at least once a month with professional service.

Common Office Cleaning Mistakes and How to Avoid Them (Especially When Receiving Clients)

Office cleaning goes beyond wiping surfaces. It's a key part of your company's image. However, many businesses make mistakes that can affect health, order, and the professional perception of the place. Here are the most frequent ones and how to avoid them.

Using Incorrect Products

Each surface requires a specific product. Using disinfectants on desks without knowing their composition can damage them or leave residues. The same applies to floors and bathrooms.

Not Cleaning "Invisible" Areas

Switches, handles, keyboards, phones, and areas under desks are often overlooked, but they accumulate bacteria and affect hygiene.

Cleaning Only When Dirt is Noticeable

Preventive cleaning avoids accumulations, odors, and germ spread. Waiting to see dirt is a reactive and inefficient approach.

Not Having a Professional Cleaning Routine

General cleaning by office staff doesn't replace professional service. Without proper equipment or training, time is wasted and deep results aren't guaranteed.

Not Having a Cleaning Checklist

A structured protocol avoids omissions. Professional companies like SGM Clean Solutions work with validated lists to ensure complete space coverage.
