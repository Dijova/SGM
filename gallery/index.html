Floor care projects in Lowell and across Massachusetts

Every photo and video in this gallery comes from a real job by SGML Clean Solutions. We strip and wax VCT and vinyl floors, burnish hallways and lobbies, deep scrub and recoat high-traffic areas, extract carpets and clean and seal tile and grout for offices, schools, stores, restaurants, apartment buildings and homes.

Commercial floor stripping & waxing

We remove old finish and buildup, then lay new coats of floor finish and burnish them for a durable, high-gloss result, scheduled at night or on weekends so your business keeps running.

Buffing, burnishing & scrub-and-recoat

Between full strips, regular burnishing and scrub-and-recoat visits restore shine and extend the life of the finish at a lower cost.

Carpet, tile & grout care

Hot-water carpet extraction and tile and grout scrubbing and sealing remove stains, odors and embedded dirt from homes and commercial spaces.

Service area: Lowell, Dracut, Chelmsford, Tewksbury, Billerica, Lawrence, Andover, Boston, Cambridge, Worcester and nearby towns in Massachusetts and southern New Hampshire (Nashua, Salem, Manchester).
